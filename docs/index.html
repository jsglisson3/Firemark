Firemark is Jeremy.

Based here in The Colony, with more than 10 years in IT and online sales. One person, start to finish: the person who builds your site is the person you talk to, and there are only a handful of clients a year.

Why the name? In 1752, Benjamin Franklin helped start America’s oldest fire insurance company. Every building it covered got a fire mark, a plaque out front that told the whole street someone had that building’s back.

Today the street is a map on a phone. Firemark makes sure your business is on it, and that someone’s looking after it.
