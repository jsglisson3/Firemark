Why “Firemark”?

In 1752, Benjamin Franklin helped start what’s now America’s oldest fire insurance company. Every building it covered got a fire mark: a plaque mounted out front where anyone could see it. It told the whole street that someone had that building’s back.

Today the street is a map on a phone. Firemark makes sure your business shows up there, and that someone’s looking after it.
