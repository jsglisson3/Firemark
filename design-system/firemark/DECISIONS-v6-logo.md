# Firemark logo, round 2 (October 26, 2026)

**Update, v6:** Jeremy picked the **flame pin**. It is now the logo everywhere, with the capital-F wordmark below. The lit pin moved to the runner-ups.

Jeremy asked to keep the F capitalized and to work on the logo as hard as it takes. Logo sheet with every draft: https://claude.ai/artifact/EVkW6EQZhk2YBQwykHD1HA

## What changed
- **Mark (v6): the flame pin** (the v3 mark A shape) with the new wordmark.
- **First recommendation, now a runner-up: the "lit pin".** A classic map pin whose top is drawn up into a curled flame tip, with an orange fire (and gold core) lit inside. It reads as a pin first, a flame second. It was meant to replace the v3 flame-pin, which reads as a flame first.
- **Wordmark: "Firemark" with a capital F.** Bricolage Grotesque at weight 640 and the text-size optical cut (opsz 24), tracking -0.02em, outlined. The old lowercase cut was 660 at opsz 32; with a capital it looked shouty.
- **Small sizes.** Under 32px the gold core is dropped (two-tone). The favicon, apple-touch and PWA icons are a charcoal tile with the pin in paper, because a bare narrow pin is weak at 16px.
- The "Site by Firemark" badges, the OG share image, the hero map pin and the brand page use the new mark.

## Explored (25 drafts, three rounds)
1. Flame shaped like a pin: the current mark, bigger fire, cut-out fire, one tip, two tongues, orange body, solid orange with a dot.
2. Starting from a real map pin: fire inside, fire cut out, fire breaking out, flame head, dot inside, orange cut-out.
3. Flame-tipped pin tuned: tip height, lean, one vs two tongues, curl, head size. K12 won.

## Runner-ups (ready in design-system/firemark/logo-variants)
- **lit-pin**: reads as a pin first, flame second. Narrow at small sizes.
- **classic-pin**: safest, reads as a pin anywhere, but the outline is generic.
- **orange-pin**: boldest one-color decal, but loses the fire.
To switch, copy that folder's files over docs/images/brand/ (and the sprite symbol paths in src/partials/sprite.html), then rebuild.
