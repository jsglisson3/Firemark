A. Main Street

Heritage sign-painting meets the fire mark plaque. Established, crafted, unmistakably local. The flame lives inside a cast plaque.

Rokkitt (slab headlines) + Libre Franklin (named for Benjamin Franklin)

B. Blue Flame

Calm, editorial, premium. The hottest part of a flame is blue, so the mark is a clean gas-flame line drawing. Lots of white space; one big sentence does the selling.

Newsreader (serif headlines) + Hanken Grotesk (body)

C. On the Map

Bold, friendly and direct. The mark is a map pin with a flame inside, and the hero literally shows a customer finding the business on a map of the lake.

Bricolage Grotesque (one family, heavy headlines)
