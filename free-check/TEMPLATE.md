# Free check template (draft)

The site promises: "Firemark looks at how your business shows up on Google today and sends a short, plain-English report," covering "where you show up, what's working and what to fix first." This is that report, plus the steps to make it in about 20 minutes. It's internal. Copy the report section for each business. The report is a personal email from Jeremy, so "I" and "me" are fine here; the website itself stays brand-first.

---

## How to run the check (about 20 minutes)

Do the searches in a private/incognito window so your own history doesn't skew results. If you can, search from near the business, or add the town to each search.

1. **Search like a customer (5 min).** Try three searches and note where the business shows up:
   - "[what they do] near me" (for example "barber near me")
   - "[what they do] The Colony"
   - The business name
   For each, note: are they in the map results (the three listings under the map)? In the regular results below? Not at all? Take a screenshot.
2. **Google Business Profile (5 min).** Search the business name and open its listing on Maps. Check: claimed? correct name, address or service area, phone, hours (including holidays)? Website link works? Main category is right? How many photos, and how recent? How many reviews, and does the owner reply?
3. **Website (5 min).** If there is one, open it on your phone:
   - Does the first screen say what they do and where?
   - Can you tap the phone number to call?
   - Does it load quickly? Run it through PageSpeed Insights (pagespeed.web.dev) and note the mobile score.
   - Does the address start with https (the padlock)?
   - Is anything out of date: hours, prices, a copyright year from years ago?
4. **Email (1 min).** Is it at their own domain (name@business.com) or a free address (Gmail, Yahoo)?
5. **Consistency (4 min).** Spot-check the name, phone and address on Apple Maps, Bing, Facebook and Yelp. Do they match Google?

Only write what you saw. No guesses about rankings or traffic, and no stats you can't point to.
